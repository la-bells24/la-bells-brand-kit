Who we are

La Bells is an online baby and kids' shop run from Islamabad. Since 2020 we've sent socks, leggings and tights, caps and gloves, clothing, swaddles and blankets, feeding sets, activities, books and toys, Ramadan and Islamic gifts, and gift sets to families across Pakistan.

How we sound

  • Warm. Like a friend who has shopped for little ones before.
  • Clear. Prices, sizes and delivery spelt out, no guessing.
  • Honest. We only say what's true.

Facts to keep straight

  • Rs. 300 delivery anywhere in Pakistan (the Rs. 250 launch offer ended on 7 November 2026). Free when products total more than Rs. 3,500. We send parcels within 1–3 working days of confirming. They usually arrive 3–5 working days after we send them.
  • Cash on delivery, or bank transfer (Meezan account or Easypaisa QR) with Rs. 75 off when the full order is paid at checkout. Paying to our WhatsApp number by JazzCash or Easypaisa is paused for now.
  • Exchange or refund within 14 days if unused. The customer pays return delivery, and we refund the item price.
  • Online only. No shop to visit, with event stalls in Islamabad and Rawalpindi now and then.
  • Gift box and handwritten card: +Rs. 950 at checkout.
  • Send later: order now, and pick the day we send it.
  • Once PostEx picks a parcel up, the customer gets a tracking link: shoplabells.com/track/ and their own code.

Writing style

  • Prices: Rs. 1,250
  • British spelling: colour, favourite
  • WhatsApp, with a capital A
  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • WhatsApp: give the number, 0333 5223557. The website footer's link reads @shoplabells; leave that one as it is.

Words we use

  • Made by La Bells: the label on things we make ourselves, nothing else.
  • The La Bells family: the sign-off under the story of something we made.
  • Bundles: Buy both and save (heading), Save Rs. 150 (sage chip), Add both (button), Bundle saving (cart line). “The Rs. 150 comes off in your cart.”

Local words

  • English word first, then the local word once, in brackets: “socks (mozay)”, “stretchy trousers (pajami)”.
  • Once per page, post or caption, not in every paragraph.
  • Exception: shop category intros and buying guides may list a few spellings people search for, once, in brackets: “Socks (bachon ke mozay, baby ki jurabain, موزے)”.
  • Any other spellings stay in the website's search words (lib/search.ts).

Never write

  • “Free returns” or “free exchanges”
  • Made-up reviews, ratings, sales numbers or stock levels
  • A shop address or opening hours
